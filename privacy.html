Privacy Policy

n8n Wisit is a personal automation used only by its owner (leewisit@gmail.com).

The app reads emails from the owner's Gmail account with read-only access to find broker trade-confirmation emails and download their attachments. The data is stored only on the owner's private server.

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. No other users' data is collected.
